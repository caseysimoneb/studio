Studio OS
SubstackCo-Design Lab

Studio OS is the agentic engineering design studio of Caseysimone Ballestas, a PhD student in mechanical engineering at UC Berkeley. Its practice is coherence engineering: the design of human-in-the-loop, auditable agentic workflows, attuned to the studio’s principal investigator (PI), that undergird any kind of project, from code to design to analysis to brainstorming.
